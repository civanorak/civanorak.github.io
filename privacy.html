Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

MindRewired is a personal automation tool that uploads videos to YouTube via the YouTube Data API.

1. Data Access

The app requests OAuth access to a YouTube account solely for uploading videos and managing the owner's own channel content.

2. Data Storage

OAuth credentials (tokens) are stored locally on the owner's computer only. No personal data is collected, shared, or sold.

3. Usage

Access tokens are used exclusively to interact with the YouTube Data API on behalf of the account owner who granted permission.
